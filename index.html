Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Wordvell 1.0

Wordvell is an English word game developed by GPolygon Design. This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

In short

Wordvell does not collect personal data. It does not ask you to create an account. It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking services. Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us. Optional purchases are made through Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your stages, scores, ink, helpers, library, daily results, sound and appearance preferences, and your profile name and picture if you add them, are kept in the app's storage on your device. They are used only to run the game and do not leave your device. The game screen does not load content from the internet and does not send this information to any server. The Android app is excluded from cloud backup. The app's internet permission is used only by the Google Play billing library for purchases.

Backups

A copy of your last good save is kept on the same device. In Settings you can download a backup file and restore it on another device. The file contains your game progress and, if you added them, your profile details. We never send a backup anywhere automatically; you decide where to keep it and whom to share it with.

Clue reports

If you report a problem with a clue, the game opens the email app on your device with a prepared message. It reaches us only if you choose to send it. The message contains the clue's details, the issue you selected, the note you typed and your email address. We use this information only to review the report and reply to you, and we do not share it with third parties. A note you prepare for a clue stays only in that session until you download it.

Links

Links on the Sources page open the related websites in your browser, where those sites' own privacy policies apply. Wordvell does not send your progress to them.

Purchases and advertising

Wordvell has no ads. The whole game is free to play. Ink packs, premium color packs and the Supporter Pack are optional one-time purchases; there are no subscriptions.

Purchases are made through Google Play Billing. Your payment details, name and email address are processed by Google and are not passed to us. The information Google uses for this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The game keeps the product you bought and the purchase number Google Play provides only in the game save on your device. This is used so that a product is never added twice and so that the packs you own come back when you reinstall; it is not sent to us. For refunds and payment questions Google Play's refund process applies; you can also write to us.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or from any other users.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all progress removes the game save and its automatic backup from this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data also removes all local saves. You can delete downloaded backup files yourself. If you would like an email you sent us to be deleted, just write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page and in the app, with the date shown above.
